# Templates & SOPs

**Templates** are ready-to-fill documents. **SOPs** (standard operating procedures) are step-by-step guides for tasks done the same way every time.

## Rules for this folder
- One template or SOP per file. The file name says the task.
- Each one lists the areas it's used in, and those areas link back.
- It also follows the rules of every area it's used in.
- Gaps to fill are marked like `{{client name}}`. Fill them from the vault, and ask about anything you can't find.
- SOPs link to other SOPs and templates instead of copying their steps.
