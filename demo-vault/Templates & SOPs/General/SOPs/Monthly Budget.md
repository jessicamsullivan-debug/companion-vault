# Monthly Budget

**Use when:** at the start of each month.

1. Gather last month's statements (never paste account numbers into the vault).
2. Note total money in and total money out.
3. Compare each spending area with the plan.
4. Note anything that went over, and why.
5. Decide one change for this month, and log it in this year's Decisions note.
