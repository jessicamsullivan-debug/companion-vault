# How I Work

## How I like information
- Bullet points, not paragraphs.
- One clear recommendation, with the reason. Options only if it's a close call.

## When I work best, and how I plan
- Best in the mornings. Creative work before lunch, admin after.
- Plans the week on Monday morning, in time blocks.

## What slows me down
- Long email threads with no clear ask.
- Switching between too many apps.
