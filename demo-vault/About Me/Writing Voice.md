# Writing Voice

Each tone has a short description, up to 3 examples, and how Alex Morgan opens and signs off. Examples have other people's names and details removed.

## Words and phrases I love
- "Brilliant", "sounds good", "let's do it"

## Words and phrases I never use
- "Synergy", "circle back", "per my last email"

## Professional
Clear and warm. Short paragraphs, the ask up front, no exclamation marks.
- Opens: "Hi Chloe," · Signs off: "Kind regards, Alex"

> Hi Chloe, thanks for sending the menu. We'll have the first two logo ideas with you by Friday. Could you let me know by Wednesday if the green needs to match your shop sign exactly? Kind regards, Alex

## Friendly
Chatty, upbeat, first names, one exclamation mark at most.
- Opens: "Hey!" · Signs off: "Speak soon, Alex"

> Hey! Loved catching up yesterday. I've popped the photos in the shared folder, have a look when you get a sec. Speak soon, Alex

## Casual
Very short. Lower case is fine. Emoji fine.
- No sign-off.

> on my way, 10 mins 👍
