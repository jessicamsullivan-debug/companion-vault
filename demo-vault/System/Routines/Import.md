# Import

1. Ask what they'd like to bring in and where it is. Only look where they point. Never search their computer on your own.
2. Copy, never move or delete, the originals into `Inbox/`. For memory from another AI, ask them to paste it into [[Inbox]].
3. Work in batches of about 10 items. For each batch, run [[Sort the Inbox]].
4. Everything imported is "not confirmed" and goes in [[To Review]], unless the person confirms it as you go.
5. Anything that clashes with the vault is raised as a contradiction.
6. After each batch, show the receipt and ask whether to carry on.
