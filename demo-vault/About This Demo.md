# About This Demo

This is an example Companion Vault for a made-up person, Alex Morgan, and a made-up business, [[Northlight Studio]]. Every person, business, price and source in it is invented, and so are the links under example.com.

Start at [[Home]], then try:
- [[Northlight Studio]] to see how a business is laid out
- [[Mum]] to see a person note, and everything that links to her
- [[To Review]] and the [[Inbox]] to see how new and uncertain things are handled
- the graph view (the dots icon on the left) to see how everything links up
