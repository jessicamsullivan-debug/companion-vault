# Northlight Studio Brand & Voice

## Tone
- Warm, clear, a little playful. Never salesy.

## Words we use
- "Independent", "local", "made with care"

## Words we never use
- "Cheap", "synergy", "solutions"

## Colours and fonts
- Deep navy and warm yellow. Headings in a rounded sans-serif.
