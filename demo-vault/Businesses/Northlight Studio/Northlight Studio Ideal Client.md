# Northlight Studio Ideal Client

## Who they are
- Independent shops, cafés and small food brands in and around Bristol.
- One to three locations, owner-run.

## What they need
- A brand that looks as good as their product, and a simple website they can update.

## Signs of a good fit
- They care how things look, and they can decide quickly.

## Signs of a poor fit
- Big chains with long sign-off chains.
