# System

These notes tell your assistant how to look after your vault. You can read them any time. To change something, just tell your assistant, for example "run the truth check every 6 months".

## Rules for this folder
- Change these notes only when the person asks, or during [[Update the Kit]] after they say yes.

## What's in here
- [[Rules]]: the core rules every assistant follows
- [[Settings]]: language, formats, routine schedule and backup
- [[Assistants]]: every assistant that uses this vault, and what it's for

### Routines
- [[Sort the Inbox]]: files everything in the Inbox (first chat each day)
- [[Close Session]]: saves everything important before you go
- [[Review Queue]]: goes through To Review with you
- [[Weekly Review]]: tasks, To Review, and planning next week
- [[Truth Check]]: checks facts that are due, and any contradictions
- [[Tidy the Map]]: fixes links and contents lists
- [[Backup Check]]: makes sure your backup is working
- [[Import]]: brings in notes and documents you already have
- [[Make a New Assistant]]: sets up another assistant that shares this vault
- [[Update the Kit]]: gets the newest version of these rules and routines

### Tools
- `Tools/vault_check.py`: the checker. It finds problems and reports them, and never changes anything.
