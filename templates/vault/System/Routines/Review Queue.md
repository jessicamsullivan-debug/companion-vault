# Review Queue

1. Open [[To Review]]. If it's empty, say so and stop.
2. Take one item at a time, oldest first. Ask one clear question.
3. With the answer: fix every note involved, update "last confirmed" and status, and log any choice in this year's Decisions note.
4. Remove the item from [[To Review]].
5. After every 5 items, ask whether to carry on or stop for now.
6. Update "Last run" in [[Settings]] and say how many items are left.

If the list reaches 10 items between reviews, mention it once at the start of a chat.
