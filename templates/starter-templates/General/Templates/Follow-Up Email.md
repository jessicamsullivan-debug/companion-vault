# Follow-Up Email

**Use when:** someone hasn't replied, and you want to nudge them.

Write it in the tone set for email in [[Communication]].

- To: `{{person}}`
- What I sent before, and when: `{{previous message}}`
- What I need from them: `{{the ask}}`
- By when: `{{deadline}}`

One short paragraph. Repeat the ask clearly, and make it easy to say yes.
