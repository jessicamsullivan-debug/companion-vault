# What's new

Your assistant reads this when you say "update the kit", and tells you what's changed.

## 1.1 (2 October 2026)

**Cleaner notes in Obsidian**
- The details at the top of each note now show a simple "source" line (told by me, web or document). Web and document sources are listed in a "Sources" section at the end of the note, with clickable links. Before, they showed as code in Obsidian.
- Each note shows its title once, instead of twice.
- **For vaults made with 1.0:** your assistant runs `System/Tools/convert_sources.py` on your vault (after you say yes), and switches off Obsidian's "Show inline title" setting. Your notes keep all their information.

**Also new since the first release**
- Building into an Obsidian vault you already have.
- Personality settings for suggesting better ideas, being a thinking partner, and being a motivator.
- When a rule or fact changes, your assistant checks the whole vault for anything that now clashes, and tidies it up.
- A "Likes and Dislikes" note in every business. Patterns there become SOPs.
- The checker spots duplicate copies made by iCloud, OneDrive or Dropbox (like "Home 2.md").
- Setup ends by putting your top one to three things in Now, so your first chat has a plan.
